How AXIOM RIFT Works

Instead of painting physical materials like glass or water, you paint different rules for distance, inertia, and time direction onto a shared 2D grid.

1. Watch Space Change Shape

Hover anywhere to inspect the local unit ball around your cursor. Right-click to drop a wave ripple and watch it shift between circular (L2), diamond (L1), and square (L∞) fronts.

2. Physics Logic Gate

Select Particle Half-Adder from the Preset menu to run 1-bit binary addition (SUM and CARRY) using particle collisions and 45 deg Hyperbolic bounce seams.

3. Controls and Shortcuts

  • Space: Play or Pause
  • 1-7: Select rule and switch to Brush
  • Right-Click: Drop a wave ripple
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y: Undo or Redo paint
